gmail-mcp

A personal tool by Lakshit Tanwar

gmail-mcp is a small program that runs on its owner's own computer. It lets the owner's AI assistant (Claude) search, read, draft and organize mail in the owner's own Gmail accounts.

It is a private, single-person tool. It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and no one other than its owner uses it.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

gmail-mcp is a personal tool used only by its owner, Lakshit Tanwar, to access his own Gmail accounts. It is not offered to anyone else.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission, granted through Google's sign-in screen, gmail-mcp accesses Gmail messages, threads, drafts and labels in the owner's own accounts, using the Gmail API scope gmail.modify.

How the data is used

The data is used only to answer the owner's own requests, for example searching, reading, drafting, labelling or archiving his email through his AI assistant.

Where the data is stored

gmail-mcp runs entirely on the owner's computer. Sign-in tokens are stored only on that computer. gmail-mcp has no server of its own, and it does not copy, store or send email data anywhere else. Email content the owner asks his AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic) to work with is processed by that assistant to fulfil his request.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

gmail-mcp's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
